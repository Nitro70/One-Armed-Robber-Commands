ONE-ARMED ROBBER: SPAWN COMMANDS
==============================================================================
All 1052 spawnable Blueprints in the game, read from the game's asset registry.

HOW TO USE (with the OARCommands mod installed)
  summon <name> [count]     e.g.  summon Goldbar_C   or   summon goldbar 10
    Works on any map: the mod loads the thing first. You can drop the _C, use a unique
    start of the name (gold), or a full /Game/... path. count copies spawn 0.15 s apart.
  dupe [count]              copy whatever is under your crosshair
  summonstop                stop a batch that is still spawning

  Without the mod, the game's own summon needs the exact class name (Goldbar_C) and only
  finds things that are already loaded on the current map.

RULES
  * Host or solo only. As a client the spawn exists on your screen only.
  * Remove something: look at it and type  DestroyTarget   (all copies:  DestroyAll <Name_C>)
  * Names ending in _base_C / Base_C are templates: they often spawn blank or broken.
  * Things built for one heist (keypads, containers, elevators) may need that map's
    other pieces to actually work.
  * OAR_Full_Object_List.txt shows which maps each thing is placed on.
